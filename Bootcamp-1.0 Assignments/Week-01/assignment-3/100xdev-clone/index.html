About Harkirat Singh (Kirat)

Harkirat Singh, popularly known as Kirat, graduated from IIT Roorkee in 2018 with a degree in Computer
Science. He has participated in Google Summer of Code twice and has worked across the technology,
finance, and startup sectors.

Harkirat has built and scaled real-world software systems. At 100xDevs, he teaches practical software
engineering, open-source development, and the skills developers need to build careers in technology.

100xDevs programs combine practical coding, hands-on projects, and open-source contributions across
full-stack development, DevOps, Web3, and AI.
